Singapore
The Heritage Pavilion

This project breathed new life into a mid-century commercial structure, transforming it into a highly functional, open-concept contemporary workspace. By reconfiguring the core layout and introducing a minimalist central courtyard, we brought natural daylight deep into the interior, fostering collaboration and productivity.

Tokyo, Japan
Residence K

Designed for a compact private apartment, this project centers on absolute spatial efficiency without sacrificing aesthetic warmth. We curated a bespoke system of floor-to-ceiling hidden storage, multifunctional minimalist furniture pieces, and selected a soft textile palette to establish a calm, clutter-free urban retreat.

Project Detail
Melbourne, Australia
Loft No. 07

A comprehensive interior overhaul of a converted industrial penthouse. By stripping away redundant partitions, we maximized spatial flow and unlocked panoramic city views. The design utilizes a warm, neutral color palette, integrated minimalist lighting, and raw structural steel accents to preserve the building's heritage while elevating modern comfort.

Ubud, Bali
The Monolith House

A brutalist-inspired residential villa crafted from raw board-formed concrete, expansive floor-to-ceiling glass, and local natural stone. The design prioritizes a seamless blur between indoor luxury and the untamed tropical landscape, creating a private sanctuary that embraces natural ventilation and passive cooling

FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions Answered

What services do you provide?

We offer full-scale architectural design, interior architecture, custom furniture styling, renovations, and complete turnkey project management.

How do you determine project pricing?

Pricing is tailored to each project based on its scale, scope of work, and complexity. We provide a detailed, transparent quote after our initial consultation.

Do you handle construction and contractors?

Yes. Through our Turnkey Project Management service, we supervise the entire build, manage trusted contractors, and handle material sourcing from start to finish.

Can I hire you for interior design only?

Absolutely. We take on standalone interior architecture and custom styling projects, as well as full structural architectural builds.

Do you offer renovation and remodeling services?

Yes. We handle renovation and remodeling projects of all sizes, helping clients refresh, improve, and transform existing spaces while maintaining quality.

How long does a typical project take to complete?

Timelines vary depending on project scope, size, and complexity. After the initial consultation, we provide a clear schedule and keep you informed.
